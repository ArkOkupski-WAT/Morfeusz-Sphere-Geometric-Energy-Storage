## Abstract
This work presents the **Morpheus Sphere Hypothesis** - a geometric model of spacetime where quasi-antimatter (qAn) forms a thin boundary layer at the edge of the Universe, serving as an energy reservoir that actively sustains the Cosmic Microwave Background and drives cosmic expansion.

## Key Features
- **Geometric ontology** of spacetime beyond ΛCDM
- **Active CMB mechanism** vs passive relic model  
- **Solution to dark energy** origin and cosmic coincidence problems
- **Energy conservation** in expanding universe explained
